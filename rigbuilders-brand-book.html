01

The Brand House

Everything Rig Builders says and builds ladders up to a single idea: peace-of-mind performance. We don't sell boxes of parts — we commission proven machines, and we stay accountable for them.

"We don't assemble PCs.
We commission them — and prove it."

Brand Idea (Essence)

Engineered confidence. A Rig Builders machine removes the doubt from buying a high-performance PC — expertly specced, built, stress-tested, documented and delivered insured, so the customer never has to wonder whether it was done right.

Vision

To make proof-built, premium custom PCs the trusted default across India — so that owning a high-performance machine is a confident, stress-free experience, never a gamble.

Mission

We commission custom PCs — expertly guided, engineered, stress-tested and documented — and deliver them insured nationwide, each one proven for exactly how its owner plays, creates or works, and backed by people who stay after the sale.

Brand Promise

Proven for exactly how you use it — guided before you buy, engineered when we build, and supported long after it ships.

Core Values

  • Proof over promises. Reliability is documented — stress tests, thermal validation, QC — not claimed.
  • Engineering discipline. Component harmony, cable management and airflow are standard, not upsells.
  • Peace of mind. We own the stress so the customer doesn't — before, during and after the purchase.
  • Premium through restraint. Quiet confidence, not hype. The work speaks; we don't shout.
  • Accountability. 3-year warranty, lifetime technical support, insured delivery — we stand behind every build.

Brand Personality

Archetype: The Engineer meets the Expert Guide (Maker × Sage). Precise, calm, confident and premium — the trusted specialist who explains, proves, and never oversells. Rig Builders is the friend who actually knows hardware and would never let you buy the wrong thing.

Brand Positioning

Positioning Statement

For discerning buyers across India who want a high-performance PC and value reliability and a premium, guided experience over the lowest price, Rig Builders is the custom-PC brand that commissions every system — expertly specced, engineered, stress-tested and documented, then delivered insured nationwide. Unlike mass-market prebuilt brands that ship assembled boxes, we hand-build and prove each machine for exactly how you use it, and stand behind it with lifetime support.

Competitive Frame

We compete against national prebuilt / custom-PC brands — not the local assembler down the street. That sets the bar high: our rivals are credible and nationwide, so our edge is experience and proof, not merely "custom."

Reasons to Believe

  • The commission process: procurement → professional assembly → stress testing → quality inspection → secure packaging → insured dispatch.
  • 3-Year system warranty, lifetime technical support and remote troubleshooting.
  • Every build hand-assembled and quality-checked by a named builder — signed, not anonymous.
  • Live compatibility, power and thermal checks in the configurator — proof starts before you buy.
  • 5.0 Google rating and real customer reviews; transparent, documented hand-off.

Price Positioning

Premium. Rig Builders sits deliberately above price-led competitors. We never win on being cheapest; we win on reliability, proof and experience. The premium is the point — it's what pays for the stress-testing, the engineering, the warranty and the human on the other end.

Audience & Segmentation

Rig Builders is not segmented by use case. A gamer, an editor and a business buyer can all be our customer — or none of them, depending on mindset. We segment by attitude.

Primary Segment — The Peace-of-Mind Premium Buyer

The person who buys from Rig Builders shares a mindset, not a job title:

  • Prefers a premium, seamless customer experience over a cheap, uncertain one.
  • Wants expert guidance — to be advised, not left to pick random parts alone.
  • Values stresslessness before, during and after the purchase.
  • Prioritises a stable, reliable system over squeezing out the last rupee of saving.
  • Is willing to spend a little more in exchange for reliability, trust and proof of the build.

Need-State Lenses (how that mindset shows up)

The same peace-of-mind buyer arrives with different jobs-to-be-done — which map to our product lines:

Play

Wants a gaming rig that just works at the resolution they play — tuned, reliable, no guesswork. → Ascend.

Create

Needs a creation machine that won't bottleneck renders or streams, built right. → Creator.

Work

Needs stability, uptime and support for professional or business workloads. → WorkPro.

Statement

Wants the flagship, bespoke best — a signature build with nothing compromised. → Signature.

Who It's For / Not For

✓ For

  • Buyers who'd rather be guided than gamble
  • People who value reliability and will pay a premium for it
  • Anyone who wants proof, not promises
  • Customers who want a brand that stays after the sale

✕ Not for

  • Pure bargain-hunters chasing the lowest price
  • Hardcore DIY tinkerers who self-build to save money
  • Buyers indifferent to support, warranty or proof
  • Anyone who sees a PC as a disposable commodity

Illustrative Personas

“Done Guessing” — the upgrader

Has built or bought before and been burned by instability, bad cable jobs or no support. Will happily pay more to never deal with that again. Wants someone to just get it right and prove it.

“Can't Afford Downtime” — the professional

Edits, renders, streams or runs a business on the machine. A crash is lost money. Buys reliability, uptime and a support line, not spec-sheet bragging rights.

“Do It Right” — the discerning enthusiast

Knows enough to care about quality, not enough to want the risk of self-building a ₹2L rig. Wants expert partnership and a build they're proud to show.

Naming Principles

  • Line names are single, confident words that signal the outcome (Ascend, Creator, WorkPro, Signature) — never spec jargon.
  • Always written as "Rig Builders [Line]" or "[Line] Series" — the masterbrand is never dropped.
  • Products within lines use the manufacturer's real model name for parts; builds get the line + tier.

✓ Do

Visual Identity

Logo

The RB monogram (hex-cut mark) sits with the "RIGBUILDERS" wordmark. Keep clear space of at least the height of the "R" around it. Use the white/monochrome wordmark on dark; the solid black wordmark on light (invoices, print). Never recolour, stretch, add effects, or place on a busy background without a scrim.

Colour — the 60 · 30 · 10 rule

Monochrome base, one accent. Matte black carries ~60% of any surface, white ~30%, and Molten Orange only ~5–10% — reserved for CTAs, active states, key figures and the logo mark. Orange is premium precisely because it's rare; it is never a wash.
