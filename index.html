How we commission your rig

STEP 01

Order lock and blueprint

Every part is checked against the rest for clearance, power and compatibility before anything is allocated to your build.

STEP 02

Precision assembly

Hand-built on the bench. Cables are routed to length, and airflow is planned around the parts you actually chose.

STEP 03

Stress testing

Sustained CPU and GPU load until temperatures settle, then benchmark runs recorded against the target you bought the machine for.

STEP 04

Proof of build

Final inspection, photographed from every side, with your test results written up and attached to the order.

STEP 05

Packed and dispatched

Foam-suspended inside the chassis, double boxed, and insured for the full value until it reaches your door.
